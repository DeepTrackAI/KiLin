# Headline (max 110 characters)

The main thing the documents show together, not a list of topics. Never
use a full stop at the end.

**Documents:** {documents}

## Scope of the Collection (80-120 words)

What the documents are about as a whole: the questions, systems and kinds
of work they cover, and what they leave out.

## Main Themes (180-260 words)

The themes the documents share, grouping the documents that address each
one and saying how they relate.

## Methods Landscape (150-220 words)

The main approaches, techniques, data and tools across the documents, and
how they differ between groups of documents.

## Key Findings (200-280 words)

The most important results, with the documents that report each, noting
numbers and conditions where the notes give them.

## Agreements and Disagreements (120-180 words)

Where the documents agree, where they conflict or differ in their results
or interpretations, and possible reasons given in the notes.

## Gaps and Open Questions (100-160 words)

What the documents do not address or leave unresolved, as stated or as
evident from what they cover.

## Where to Start Reading (3-5 items)

The documents that give the best entry to the collection, chosen from
{key_documents}.
