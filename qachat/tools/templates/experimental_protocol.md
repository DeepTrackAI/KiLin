# Headline (max 110 characters)

Name the experiment or measurement and the system studied.

**Source paper:** {citation}

## Objective and Scope (80-120 words)

State what the procedure was designed to measure or establish and the
experimental context in which the authors used it.

## Materials and Apparatus (120-200 words)

Extract reported materials, specimens, instruments, software, and essential
configurations. Mark missing identifiers, quantities, and specifications
explicitly as not reported.

## Preparation and Preconditions (120-200 words)

Describe sample preparation, calibration, environmental conditions, and
prerequisites only to the extent documented in the paper.

## Reported Procedure (250-380 words)

Reconstruct the reported order of operations faithfully. Include timings,
settings, repetitions, and decision points where specified. Label any
missing step as unspecified; do not invent executable instructions.

## Controls and Quality Checks (120-200 words)

Extract negative and positive controls, calibration checks, acceptance
criteria, and failure handling when stated. Identify absent reporting.

## Measurements and Analysis (160-240 words)

Explain collected outputs, data processing, calculations, and uncertainty
analysis, preserving reported units and parameter definitions.

## Missing Information and Verification Needs (130-200 words)

Identify details required to reproduce or safely execute the experiment
that are not established by this source. Distinguish reported facts from
inferences. Require consultation of supplementary methods, applicable
safety procedures, and original authors before laboratory execution.

## Related Reviews and Literature (2–5 items)

The papers or reviews that give readers the best context, not the paper
itself. Chosen from {references}.
