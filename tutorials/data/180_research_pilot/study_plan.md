# Microscopy tracking pilot — study plan

**Status:** Fictional teaching dataset; not an actual experiment.
**Study ID:** TRACK-PILOT-01. Values in these notes are illustrative.

## Research question and acquisition

A pilot compared automated particle tracking with manual tracking on the
same twelve fluorescence microscopy movies. Six movies had sparse particles
and six had crowded fields. Each movie was recorded at 10 frames per second
for 120 seconds with a 20x objective. The comparison was restricted to a
single acquisition batch.

## Measurement protocol

Both arms received the same unmodified movies. A manually reviewed subset
provided the reference positions used to estimate localization error. The
pilot tracked trajectories processed per minute, median localization error
in pixels, and the percentage of attempted trajectories rejected during
quality control (QC). It did not measure biological outcomes.
