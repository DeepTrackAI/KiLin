# Microscopy tracking pilot — manual arm

**Status:** Fictional teaching dataset; not an actual experiment.
**Study ID:** TRACK-PILOT-01.

## Manual tracking method

Two trained annotators tracked the same movies used by the automated arm.
The reviewed reference positions were prepared separately from the
reported annotations. Both annotators followed the same trajectory-length
and motion-consistency QC definitions.

## Manual tracking measurements

Manual tracking throughput was 70 trajectories per minute, measured as
completed annotations. Median localization error relative to the reviewed
positions was 0.11 pixels. The manual QC rejection rate was 5 percent of
attempted trajectories. These figures describe the illustrative pilot only.
