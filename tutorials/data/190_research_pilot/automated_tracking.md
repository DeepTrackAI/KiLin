# Microscopy tracking pilot — automated arm

**Status:** Fictional teaching dataset; not an actual experiment.
**Study ID:** TRACK-PILOT-01.

## Automated tracking method

The automated arm used a fixed particle detector and linked detections
across frames. A trajectory was rejected when it failed the predefined
minimum-length or motion-consistency QC checks. No parameters were tuned
on the evaluation movies.

## Automated tracking measurements

Automated tracking throughput was 210 trajectories per minute, measured
as completed annotations rather than camera acquisition speed. Median
localization error relative to reviewed positions was 0.18 pixels.
The automated QC rejection rate was 14 percent of attempted trajectories.
These figures describe the illustrative pilot only.
