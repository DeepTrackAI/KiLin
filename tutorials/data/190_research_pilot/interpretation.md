# Microscopy tracking pilot — interpretation and limitations

**Status:** Fictional teaching dataset; not an actual experiment.
**Study ID:** TRACK-PILOT-01.

## Comparison

The recorded annotation throughput ratio was 210/70 = 3, so the automated
arm processed three times as many trajectories per minute in this example.
The automated arm had higher median localization error (0.18 versus 0.11
pixels) and higher QC rejection (14 versus 5 percent). Throughput alone
therefore does not establish overall method superiority.

## Limitations and next experiment

There was no external validation dataset, no confidence interval or formal
uncertainty analysis, and only one acquisition batch. The reference positions
were manually reviewed, so review bias is possible. A next experiment
should use independent movies, blinded reference annotations, and uncertainty
estimates across acquisition batches. No clinical or biological performance
claim is supported by this pilot.
